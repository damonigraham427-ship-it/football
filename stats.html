Football — Physical Stats of the Environment

Sources: 2025 NFL Rulebook and NCAA Football Rules (Rule 1). Imperial and metric. Lines marked COMPUTED are calculated from the official figures, not official stats.

1. The Ball — NFL

Shape: prolate spheroid — Rule 2, Section 1

Long axis (length): 11 to 11-1/4 in (27.94 to 28.58 cm)

Long circumference: 28 to 28-1/2 in (71.12 to 72.39 cm)

Short circumference: 21 to 21-1/4 in (53.34 to 53.98 cm)

Weight: 14 to 15 oz (396.9 to 425.2 g)

Inflation pressure: 12-1/2 to 13-1/2 psi (86.2 to 93.1 kPa)

Material / construction: inflated urethane bladder enclosed in a pebble-grained leather case, natural tan color, without corrugations of any kind; ball must be a "Wilson," hand selected, bearing the Commissioner's signature

Stripes: none specified (plain leather case)

Average density: approx. 0.095 g/cm³ (0.0952 at mid-range dimensions and weight; full tolerance range approx. 0.090 to 0.101 g/cm³) — COMPUTED. Prolate spheroid volume at mid-range ≈ 263.4 in³ (4,316 cm³) at 14.5 oz (411.1 g). Estimate only — a real ball is a hollow shell, so this is the average density of the whole ball including its air.

1. The Ball — NCAA (differs from NFL where noted)

Shape: prolate spheroid — Rule 1-1-1-a and Rule 1-3-1-f diagram

Long diameter (length): 10-7/8 to 11-7/16 in (27.62 to 29.05 cm) — slightly wider tolerance than NFL, can be shorter — Rule 1-3-1-f ball diagram (see Flags)

Long circumference: 27-3/4 to 28-1/2 in (70.49 to 72.39 cm) — minimum 1/4 in smaller than NFL — diagram (see Flags)

Short circumference: 20-3/4 to 21-1/4 in (52.71 to 53.98 cm) — minimum 1/4 in smaller than NFL — diagram (see Flags)

Weight: 14 to 15 oz (396.9 to 425.2 g) — same as NFL — Rule 1-3-1-h

Inflation pressure: 12-1/2 to 13-1/2 psi (86.2 to 93.1 kPa) — same as NFL — Rule 1-3-1-g

Material / construction: cover of four panels of pebble-grained leather without corrugations other than seams; one set of eight equally spaced lacings; natural tan color — Rule 1-3-1-b, c, d

Stripes: two 1-inch (2.54 cm) white stripes, 3 to 3-1/4 in (7.62 to 8.26 cm) from the end of the ball, only on the two panels adjacent to the laces — Rule 1-3-1-e

Average density: approx. 0.096 g/cm³ (0.0961 at mid-range; full range approx. 0.088 to 0.104 g/cm³) — COMPUTED. Mid-range volume approx. 261.0 in³ (4,277 cm³) at 14.5 oz (411.1 g).

2. The Field — shared dimensions (both leagues)

Total length, end line to end line (incl. end zones): 360 ft / 120 yd (109.73 m)

Width, sideline to sideline: 160 ft / 53-1/3 yd (48.77 m)

Field of play, goal line to goal line: 100 yd (91.44 m)

End zone depth (goal line to end line): 10 yd / 30 ft (9.14 m) each end

Total field area: 57,600 sq ft (5,351 m²) — COMPUTED from 360 ft × 160 ft

Yard lines: across the field of play at 5-yard intervals (4.57 m), parallel to the goal lines

Yard line width: 4 in (10.16 cm)

Goal line width: 8 in (20.32 cm) — NFL; 4 or 8 in (10.16 or 20.32 cm) — NCAA

Sideline / end line width: 4 in (10.16 cm) minimum; may exceed 4 in — NCAA

All other lines width: 4 in (10.16 cm); in the NFL the goal line and the yellow restraining lines are the 8-inch exceptions

Measurements: from the inside edges of the boundary lines; the entire width of the goal line is in the end zone

2. The Field — hash marks (the big league difference)

NFL — distance from each sideline: 70 ft 9 in (21.56 m)

NFL — spacing between the two rows: 18 ft 6 in (5.64 m) — same as the goalpost width

NFL — hash mark size: 4 in wide × 2 ft long (10.16 cm × 60.96 cm)

NCAA — distance from each sideline: 60 ft (18.29 m)

NCAA — spacing between the two rows: 40 ft (12.19 m)

NCAA — hash mark length: 24 in (60.96 cm)

2. The Field — other markings and surface facts

Border: solid white border a minimum of 6 ft (1.83 m) wide along the end lines and sidelines — NFL

Field numerals: 2 yd (1.83 m) in length, bottoms beginning 12 yd (10.97 m) in from each sideline — NFL

Field numerals: not larger than 6 ft high × 4 ft wide (1.83 m × 1.22 m), tops 9 yd (8.23 m) from the sidelines (recommended) — NCAA

Directional arrows: triangle with two sides of 36 in (91.44 cm) and an 18 in (45.72 cm) base, pointing toward the nearest goal line — both leagues

Playing surface: the surface of the entire field of play must be a League-approved shade of green — NFL

Playing surface: no material or device shall be used to improve or degrade the playing surface to give one player or team an advantage — NCAA

Line marking material: all lines marked with a material not injurious to eyes or skin — NFL

Benches / rigid fixtures: none nearer than 30 ft (9.14 m) from the sidelines — NFL

3. The Goalposts

Crossbar height (top face of crossbar above ground): 10 ft (3.05 m) — both leagues

Crossbar length / inside width between uprights: 18 ft 6 in (5.64 m) — both leagues

Upright height above crossbar: 35 ft (10.67 m); total goal height 45 ft (13.72 m) — total COMPUTED — NFL

Upright height: uprights extend at least 30 ft (9.14 m) above the ground, as worded in the rule (see Flags) — NCAA

Upright diameter: no less than 3 in and no more than 4 in (7.62 to 10.16 cm) — NFL

Post type and color: single-standard type, offset from the end line, bright gold — NFL

Post placement: inside of the uprights and crossbar in the same vertical plane as the inside edge of the end line; uprights and crossbar white or yellow — NCAA

Padding: posts "padded in a manner prescribed by the League" (no numeric height published — see Flags) — NFL

Padding: goal posts padded with resilient material from the ground to at least 6 ft (1.83 m) — NCAA

Wind ribbon / streamers: orange ribbon 4 in × 42 in (10.16 cm × 106.68 cm) at the top of each post — NFL

Wind streamers: 4 in × 42 in (10.16 cm × 106.68 cm) orange or red streamers at the top of the uprights are permitted — NCAA

4. Pylons, limit lines, line-to-gain equipment

Pylons (NCAA): soft, flexible, four-sided, 4 in × 4 in (10.16 cm × 10.16 cm) cross-section, overall height 18 in (45.72 cm), which may include a 2 in (5.08 cm) space between the bottom of the pylon and the ground; red or orange — Rule 1-2-6

Pylon placement (NCAA): at the inside corners of the eight intersections of the sidelines with the goal lines and end lines; pylons at the intersections of the end lines and hash marks extended are placed 3 ft (0.91 m) off the end lines

Pylons (NFL): weighted pylons mark the four intersections of the goal lines and sidelines at the inside corners, plus two pylons on each end line — placement and weighting stated, no dimensions published (see Flags)

Limit lines (NCAA): 12 ft (3.66 m) outside the sidelines and end lines (never less than 6 ft / 1.83 m where stadium space does not permit); 4 in (10.16 cm) wide, may be yellow

Restraining (limit) line (NFL): a broken limit line 6 ft (1.83 m) outside the 6 ft white border encompasses the field in the non-bench areas

Yardage chain (NCAA): two rods not fewer than 5 ft (1.52 m) high, the rods' inside edges exactly 10 yd (9.14 m) apart with the chain fully extended; operated approx. 6 ft (1.83 m) outside the sideline

Down indicator (NCAA): mounted on a rod not fewer than 5 ft (1.52 m) high

Sideline markers (NFL): the home club provides the standard set approved by the Commissioner (no dimensions published in Rule 1)

Flags — read with care

NCAA ball dimensions come from the official rule diagram (Rule 1-3-1-f defers to it), cross-checked across published reproductions — diagram values, not rule-text values.

NCAA upright height follows the rule text as written: "at least 30 feet above the ground" (Rule 1-2-5-a). Secondary summaries often restate it as "above the crossbar" — that is not what the rule says.

NFL pylon dimensions are not published in the rulebook; the 4 in × 4 in × 18 in figure is the NCAA spec, not an NFL one.

NFL goal post padding height is not published in the rulebook; the NCAA's at-least-6 ft figure should not be assumed for the NFL.

Surface material: neither rulebook mandates a material by name — the NFL states a League-approved shade of green; the NCAA article governs tampering and field condition.

NCAA ball bladder material is not named in the rule; the NFL rule specifies a urethane bladder.
